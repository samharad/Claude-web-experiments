# Arthur's Age

Shows how long baby Arthur has been alive.

## Spec

- Arthur was born July 26, 2026 at 8:48 AM Pacific time (PDT, UTC-7); the birth moment is hardcoded with its UTC offset so the age is correct in any viewer timezone.
- Displays his age two ways:
  - Total days
  - Weeks + days (e.g. "1 week, 5 days")
- Also shows a live-updating line with hours, minutes, and seconds into the current day of life.
- No inputs, no storage — just open the page and see the age.
